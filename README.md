## 💫 About Me

I'm an **Electronics and Communication Engineering** graduate from **Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Bangladesh**, with a strong passion for software engineering and modern technologies.

My engineering background has shaped my problem-solving mindset, analytical thinking, and interest in building reliable technology solutions. I enjoy developing full-stack web applications, designing backend architectures, working with databases, and exploring emerging technologies.
